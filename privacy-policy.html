Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 Nov 2026

Hijazi Moto Parts (the "App") is operated by Ali Dbouk (trading as A.D) ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. Contact: ali.dbouk2511@gmail.com

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, phone number and city, which you enter when you create an account. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see or store it.
  • Orders: when you tap Buy, we record the item, its price, the exchange rate, your account ID, name and phone number, and the time of the order, so that we can match your payment and show your order status.
  • Repair shop listings: if you register a repair shop, we collect the shop name, phone number, a map location link and your account ID. Once a shop is approved, its name, phone number and map position are visible to all users of the App.
  • Technical data: our service providers (Google) may process technical data such as IP address and device information to deliver and secure their services.

The App does not read your device location, contacts, photos or microphone.

2. How we use information

We use your information to create and manage your account, to process and track your orders, to review, list and renew repair shops, to contact you about your orders or listings, to keep the App secure and prevent abuse, and to meet legal obligations.

3. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We use Google Firebase (sign-in and database) and Google Maps or Apple Maps (to display maps). When you tap Buy or register a shop, the App opens WhatsApp, SMS or Whish Money with a pre-filled message. What you send there is handled by those services under their own policies, and we receive it as the recipient. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

4. Where data is stored

Data is stored on Google Firebase servers, which may be located outside Lebanon. By using the App you understand that your data may be processed in other countries.

5. How long we keep information

Account and profile data are kept while your account is active. Order records may be kept after you delete your account when needed for accounting and dispute handling. Repair shop listings are kept while they are active and can be removed on request.

6. Your choices and rights

You can see your name, email and orders inside the App (My Account). You can delete your account inside the App (My Account, then Delete my account). This deletes your profile and your sign-in. Order records may be kept as described above. To ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or removal of a shop listing, contact ali.dbouk2511@gmail.com.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and Firebase security rules to protect your data. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
